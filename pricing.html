Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How is the per-bed pricing calculated?
Your per-bed price is the sum of the modules you enable, and your monthly cost is that per-bed price × the number of beds registered in your PG. For example, an 80-bed PG on Core + Announcements + Meter + Complaints (₹26/bed) pays ₹2,080 per month. All registered beds are counted, whether or not they're occupied. Use the calculator above to model your exact setup.
How do the 1, 2 and 3-year terms save me money?
The longer you commit, the more months you get free. A 1-year plan includes 2 free months (you're billed for 10 of 12), a 2-year plan includes 5 free months (billed for 19 of 24), and a 3-year plan includes 8 free months (billed for 28 of 36). The calculator shows the exact effective monthly rate, total and savings for each term. Managing a larger PG? You may also qualify for a special volume offer — just talk to our team.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes! We offer a free onboarding and setup period to help you get started. Contact us directly via email or phone and we'll walk you through the platform before you commit. We want you to be confident before paying a single rupee.
Can I change my modules later?
Absolutely. You can switch modules on or off at any time, and your per-bed price simply recalculates as the sum of what's currently enabled. As your PG grows and you add beds, you're only ever billed for the beds registered on your account — never for capacity you don't use.
Do residents need to pay anything?
No. The Resident App is completely free for your residents to download and use. There are no charges for residents. Only the PG owner pays the subscription, and only for the beds they manage.
Is my data secure on Room Rover?
Absolutely. All your data is encrypted, stored securely in the cloud, and backed up automatically. We use industry-standard security practices. Your resident data, payment records, and property information are private and protected at all times.
Can I use Room Rover on my laptop or PC?
Yes! Room Rover includes a full web dashboard that works on any browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox. Whether you're on a laptop, desktop, or tablet, you get the same powerful management experience. The Android app is available on Play Store for mobile use.
