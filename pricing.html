Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

How is the per-bed pricing calculated?
Pricing is based on the total number of beds registered in your PG. For example, if you have 80 beds, you fall under the Growth plan at ₹32/bed — so your monthly cost is ₹2,560. The more beds you have, the lower your per-bed rate. All beds in your PG are counted, regardless of whether they're occupied.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes! We offer a free onboarding and setup period to help you get started. Contact us directly via email or phone and we'll walk you through the platform before you commit. We want you to be confident before paying a single rupee.
What happens if my PG grows and crosses a tier?
Great news — your price per bed actually goes down as you grow! When you cross a tier threshold (e.g., from 50 to 51 beds), your entire account moves to the new, lower per-bed rate. You are automatically rewarded for scaling with Room Rover.
Do residents need to pay anything?
No. The Resident App is completely free for your residents to download and use. There are no charges for residents. Only the PG owner pays the subscription, and only for the beds they manage.
Is my data secure on Room Rover?
Absolutely. All your data is encrypted, stored securely in the cloud, and backed up automatically. We use industry-standard security practices. Your resident data, payment records, and property information are private and protected at all times.
Can I use Room Rover on my laptop or PC?
Yes! Room Rover includes a full web dashboard that works on any browser — Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox. Whether you're on a laptop, desktop, or tablet, you get the same powerful management experience. The Android app is available on Play Store for mobile use.
